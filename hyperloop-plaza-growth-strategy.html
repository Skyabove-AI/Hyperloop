It isn't a funnel. It's a loop.

A funnel ends. A loop comes back around: attention becomes a visit, a visit becomes a habit, and each guest recruits the next, so every turn lowers acquisition cost and raises lifetime value.

Each full turn lowers acquisition cost and raises lifetime value.

X virality, press, and the Musk halo do the hard part for free. We amplify and steer it with local SEO, Maps, answer-engine optimization, and tourism partners, so attention points at a destination, not a dead end.
