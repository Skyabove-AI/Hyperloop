## 📋 The Plan

A comprehensive growth strategy to transform Hyperloop Plaza from four separate small businesses into **one self-compounding destination loop** — where every visit feeds the next and lifetime value compounds with each turn.

### Strategy Overview

| Phase | Focus | Timeline |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Ground Truth** | Audit, baseline metrics, unified brand identity | Days 1–14 |
| **Workflows** | Local habit engine, loyalty, email/SMS capture | Days 15–30 |
| **Systems** | Destination & merch engine, automation, paid media | Days 31–45 |
| **Compound** | Loop accelerates — systems run themselves | Days 46–60 |

### Core Pillars

1. **One Destination, One Front Door** — Unified brand, single website, one Google listing
2. **The Local Habit Engine** — Loyalty mechanics, weekly rituals, and regulars programs for Bastrop & Boring Co. employees
3. **The Destination & Merch Engine** — Out-of-market visitors, SpaceX/Boring Co. tourists, and branded merchandise
4. **The Engine Room** — Analytics, automation, and the systems that make the loop self-sustaining

### Four Target Audiences

| Audience | Who | Core Need |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| **The Builders** | Boring Co. / SpaceX / X employees | Quick lunch, after-work drinks, convenience |
| **Bastrop Locals** | Families, retirees, small-town regulars | A reason to come back every week |
| **The Pilgrims** | Tech tourists, Elon fans, out-of-towners | The "I went to Boring Company's plaza" story |
| **Groups & Events** | Corporate offsites, birthday parties, teams | Bookable experiences and private space |

### Key Metrics

- **Visit frequency** — Repeat visits per customer per month
- **Owned audience** — Email/SMS list growth
- **Out-of-market revenue** — Merch + tourist spend as % of total
- **Cost per acquired visit** — Paid media efficiency

## 🏗 About Hyperloop Plaza

**Hyperloop Plaza** is a community plaza at **865 FM 1209 in Bastrop, Texas** — about 30 minutes east of Austin, near The Boring Company, SpaceX, and X facilities.

### What's There

- ☕ **Boring Bodega** — Coffee, groceries, grab-and-go
- 🍺 **Prufrock Pub** — Craft beer, cocktails, food
- 💇 **His & Hers Salon** — Haircuts, nails, grooming
- 🎨 **Cafe Monet** — Weekend pottery painting for all ages
- 🏓 **Courts & Playground** — Pickleball, kids playground, candy shop

**Open to the public. Free parking. Family friendly.**
