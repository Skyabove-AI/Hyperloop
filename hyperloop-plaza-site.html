A glimpse of the future, with a small-town welcome.

Built by The Boring Company on the edge of Bastrop, Hyperloop Plaza is a bodega, a pub, a salon, a pottery studio, and pickleball, all under one roof. Come for the curiosity. Stay for the coffee.

Plan your visit

Make a day of it.

The Plaza is built for lingering. Here's one easy way to spend a few hours, morning to evening.

MORNING

Coffee & a slow start

Grab a coffee and a pastry at Boring Bodega, then let the kids burn off energy at the playground.

MIDDAY

Lunch & a match

Lunch at Prufrock Pub, then book a pickleball court and play a few games in the Texas sun.

AFTERNOON

Make something

Paint your own pottery at Cafe Monet on the weekend, or freshen up with a cut at His & Hers Salon.

EVENING

Wind down

Cocktails and dinner back at Prufrock, and a stop at the candy shop for the road home.
